* Evaluation metrics
    - `FacetCoverage@k` = Number of retrieved facets / Total number of facets
    - **MAIN: `FacetCoveragePurity@k`** = `FacetCoverage@k` * `Precision@k`, combining our two goals: retrieving more query facets while avoiding distractors/outliers.
    - `AllFacetCleanRate@k` = Percentage of queries that have `FacetCoverage@k` == 1 && `Precision@k` >= clean_rate_threshold (0.80)

* Decoupled the FacLoc and MMR lambda values
    - Due to its mathematical nature, the FacLoc scoring function stars diversifying only when lambda < 0.40
    - MMR starts diversifying even at higher values of lambda (less MMR weight)

* Show results:
    - Smaller datasets seem to not have the desired advantage for FacLoc, MMR can perform slightly better for certain data distributions.
    - Bigger datasets with heavier data masses and a higher number of distractors/outliers tend to improve the FacLoc scores, while MMR struggles due to the distractors.
    - The data distribution has a great impact on which method performs better

    - In una pipeline reale, gli step sarebbero:
        * pre-filtering con K_pre >> k
        * esecuzione dei metodi di diversificazione sul set prefiltrato

    - Un metodo che performa meglio in generale è più robusto e più facile da mantenere, meno soggetto alla distribuzione dei dati e al contenuto semantico dei documenti che recupera.
