## Facet Labels

- **Good labels** (broad, reusable): `drug_choice`, `dose_adjustment`, `monitoring_frequency`, `contraindication`, `complication_management`, `discharge_planning`.
- **Bad labels** (too specific): `insulin_dose_reduction_for_ckd`, `metformin_contraindication`, `creatinine_monitoring`.
- Goal: labels should be varied and general, not all clinical micro-details --> check how to extend gold labels and bad labels with more diverse points of view, not only medical (e.g. also family history and such?).

## Gold Evaluation Approach

- **Current facet-based approach** (aspect coverage) is an internal verification only — it is not sufficient to claim coverage > top-k.
- **Final evaluation** must be based on **gold answers**: generate a reference answer per query, then measure token overlap between that answer and the concatenation of chunks returned by top-k / coverage / dispersion.
- **Caveat:** MIMIC is not designed for QA — generating gold answers that are actually grounded in the discharge notes is non-trivial. This tension needs to be addressed.

## Scale

- **~500 queries total** is feasible, including some degree of human supervision/spot annotation. More is not practical.
